## Playbook

- Put the job in the name on TikTok (Cal AI - Calorie Tracker, BitePal: Food Calorie Tracker). Keep Instagram as Zest. Picture is the character, small enough to sit inside the circle.
- Bio is two or three short lines. One line says what happens. One line is the personality or the proof. End with 👇 or “Link is here ⬇️”. Do not invent a user count.
- Group the profile the way Yazio groups playlists: Log, Meals, Diary, Guide, Streak, Lemon.
- The video that wins starts with words, then the proof, inside two seconds. Cal AI shows the scan. Zest should show the plate, the number, then the lemon.
- Repeat one format. Cal AI’s recent posts are the same face-and-phone video, and those are the ones with hundreds of thousands of views.
- Captions are the hook again, then four or five tags. Not a paragraph, not thirty hashtags.
- Pin the video that explains the snap once it is up. BitePal pins theirs.
- Post a few times a week for a long time. Yazio’s biggest posts are spread from 2024 to 2026.
