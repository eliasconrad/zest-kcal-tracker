# App research for accounts, friends and recipes (October 30, 2026)

Read in Appllama as screen metadata. Screens are described, never copied.

## Accounts

- Cal AI, MyFitnessPal, fatsecret and SnapCalorie all ask for the account in onboarding. Cal AI and fatsecret offer Apple, Google and e-mail on one sheet, Apple first. SnapCalorie forces registration and e-mail verification before a single question, the worst of the set.
- MyFitnessPal's settings live behind a More tab as a flat list.
- **Zest:** the diary works without an account (a guest session is created quietly), the account step comes after the plan and can be skipped, Apple is on top. An account buys sync and friends. A guest who signs up keeps everything.

## Friends

- YAZIO shows buddies inside the profile; BitePal has challenges with coins and progress bars; Finch adds a friend through a shared invitation with a toast; Duolingo has a weekly leaderboard and friend streaks; Calo and fatsecret have nothing comparable or a public community feed.
- Public feeds and coins do not fit a diary about what someone eats. Sharing a food diary with others can also hurt people with a difficult relationship to food.
- **Zest:** invite by link only (Finch), a small friends list in the profile with each friend's streak and whether they logged today (Duolingo), a weekly strip of who logged most days. Calories stay private unless shared per friend. No public profiles, no ranking by weight or calories.

## Recipes

- YAZIO has a discover tab with categories, recommendation grids, filters by diet, favourites and ingredient exclusion. Fitatu sells whole meal plans with calorie badges on photos and a results chart.
- Whole meal plans are a product of their own and out of scope for now.
- **Zest:** a searchable sheet, diet and allergy filters taken from the onboarding, a detail with a servings stepper that recalculates, Log this, favourites, and Suggest a recipe from the model based on what is left today. Drawn stickers instead of photos, because stock photos would look borrowed.
